# v0.6: Gemma on the phone

**Date:** 18 Nov 2026

In v0.5 the practice companion ran on the laptop's GPU, so every question went to the studio's server. Gemma 4 E2B
now runs on the student's own phone with LiteRT-LM for Android. The chat, and the health notes it keeps, never leave
the phone unless the student shares one.

## What was built

- **The model is an opt-in download.** It is 2.6 GB, so Ask offers it with Wi-Fi recommended.
  - It comes from the studio's copy on Google Drive, with Hugging Face as the fallback.
  - Android's DownloadManager carries it, so the download survives the app closing.
  - Its size and SHA-256 are checked before the first start.
- **Starting it.** Gemma starts on the GPU where a phone has one, else on the CPU. A GPU start that crashed the app is
  not tried again.
  - A phone with less memory than a 6 GB phone is told that chat needs more memory and that cloud model support is
    coming soon, in all three languages.
  - The answers from the notes alone, without Gemma, are gone.
- **The companion moved onto the phone:** the search over the studio's notes, the emergency check (the fixed reply
  with 112 and Tele-MANAS 14416, before any model runs), the "Remember that…" save, the system message and the tools.
  - The tools ask the server only for what is the student's own: the practice summary (`GET /me/practice`) and the
    coming classes (`GET /home`).
  - The studio's notes and `studio.json` are copied into the app at build time.
- **Decoding.** Greedy, with an 8-token repeat ban over 16 tokens (the v0.5 fix that lets a list say "8:00" on each
  line). Marathi answers may not use other scripts' letters or digits, from a bundled 32 KB token list.
- **The chat and the notes stay on the phone,** one file per account. On Me, each health note has "Share with" the
  teacher (confirmed first) and "Forget". The teacher sees shared notes on Students (`POST /me/shared`,
  `GET /teacher/shared`).

## Measured

On the 6 GB Android emulator, on the CPU:

| Step | Time |
|---|---|
| Download from the laptop | 95 s |
| SHA-256 check | 7 s |
| A reply | 7 to 13 s |

The conversations of v0.5, run again with E2B on the laptop's GPU through the companion's new prompt, passed 20 of 21
automatic checks ([every answer](../evals/companion_v0.6_e2b.md)). The one miss, "hi-chip-tomorrow", gave tomorrow's
class right, then listed the classes of the two days after. The first probe of Gemma with tools, before v0.5 was
built, is in [`companion_probe`](../evals/companion_probe.md).

## Privacy

- The chat and the health notes are on the phone. The studio's server never sees them, and nothing goes to an outside
  AI service.
- A note reaches the teacher only when the student shares it, one note at a time.
- Me says this in one line: what stays on the phone, and what the teacher sees.
