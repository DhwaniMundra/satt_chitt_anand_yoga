# v1.0: a practice tracker for the studio

**Date:** 19 Nov 2026

v0.7 put the backend on a Google Sheet. v1.0 makes the app what the studio needs: a record of each student's
practice, asana by asana, in class and on their own, which the teacher reads to guide them. Students practise in
batches, and the teacher places each new student in one from what they say about themselves. Gemma on the phone
answers questions about all of it. The release APK talks to the live web app.

## For a student

- **Joining.** The first screen shows the studio's blessing and the choice of language. A student signs up with the
  group code from the class's WhatsApp invite, then tells the teacher about themselves: age, height, weight, food
  habits, any pain, and how six asanas feel today, each with how to try it safely. A line before Send says the
  answers go to the teacher, to place them in a batch. Until then the app says "Waiting for Ramit ji".
- **Today** names the student's batch and shows only its classes and Meet links. The next class shows its sequence.
  Join, say how you feel, and after class say how it went:
  - the mood;
  - each item's reaction: not yet, with effort, steady, at ease;
  - "felt strain or pain", and a note.

  Everything can be skipped, and the answers can be changed for 12 hours.
- **Practice** has the totals, then "How each item has gone". Each asana shows:
  - times in class and on one's own;
  - how it felt at first against lately;
  - strain or pain;
  - whether the teacher wrote.

  An item opens its history: every class and solo entry, and the teacher's feedback.
- **Practise on your own.** Repeat your last class, a whole sequence, or items from the library. Each item has a
  stopwatch, then the same reaction, strain and note. The session shows in each item's history.
- **Ask** is Gemma on the phone ([v0.6](v0.6.md)), with a sixth tool, `asana_progress`. "How is my Setu Bandhasana
  going?" gets that item's sums, the teacher's latest word and the student's own notes. One suggestion asks about the
  student's own item that most needs attention, and Gemma knows the student's batch.
- **Me:** the health notes, kept on the phone and shareable with the teacher one at a time; "About me"; the privacy
  line; language; feedback to the studio.

## For the teacher

- **Today** has one card per batch for the next day with classes left. Each card has:
  - the curriculum, chosen with one tap among the batch's choices;
  - the sequence;
  - the Meet link to share on WhatsApp.

  A card for students waiting to be placed opens Students.
- **Students.**
  - A waiting student's card shows their answers and the batch they point to, preselected: "Approve into Pain
    management".
  - A placed student shows "Intermediate · since Sat 14 Nov" and "Move to batch", with a note kept as review
    history.
  - Each student's items open to their history, with a box for feedback.
- **Insights:** students, mood lift and shared notes. Each class opens to its review: reactions per item, with strain
  or pain first, names and notes.

## The backend

Every route the app uses is in `appsscript/`, tested on Node fakes of the Sheet with the clock frozen. There are 92
tests, swept over 22 moments of the week.

- **New tabs:** the library and curricula (seeded once, then the owner's to edit), practice, solo sessions, asana
  feedback, batches and their changes, and the intake (hidden).
- **The Dashboard adds:** strain flags, reactions per item over time, students per batch, batch changes, and pain
  areas as counts.
- **Growth:** about 1,300 practice rows a week; the README has the yearly archive step.

Upgrading the live Sheet: [`appsscript/README.md`](../../appsscript/README.md#update-later).

## A fresh review

Before the release, a reviewer with no part in building it read the whole code. The fixes:

- **Gemma's download.** After a failed download, the fallback address started, but nothing followed it to the end.
- **A student moved during the 12 hours after a class** could not give the mood after it, and the app's answers about
  the items were lost with it.
- **A waiting student's Practice tab** failed twice on opening. It now shows the waiting card, and Ask's pending line
  no longer points to the removed way of entering a group code.
- **A removed student** was told they were waiting, and was offered a class to say how it went. They now see one line
  and Log out, and the server sends anyone not approved nothing about classes.
- **Students abroad.** A Monday 7:00 class in India was "Tomorrow" on a Sunday evening in New York. Every day is now
  named on the student's own calendar, and Gemma's schedule too.
- **Signed in but offline at start**, the app showed the sign-up form. It now says the studio could not be reached,
  and tries again.
- **The intake's rule** let three of four steady poses point to Intermediate. It takes four.
- **Text was cleared before a send went through**, and a new password was made without asking. A failed send now
  keeps the text, and a new password needs a confirm.
- **Every sub-screen has a back arrow**, and a tab tapped again goes back to its first screen.
- **The rest:** Marathi dates in Devanagari digits like the app's other numbers; the stale lines Gemma read ("before
  8 AM"); the brand's spelling; fields nothing read; history in comments; the teacher's name in code.

## The phone evaluation

[`companion_v1_phone`](../evals/companion_v1_phone.md) has 21 conversations in English, Hindi and Marathi, asked in
the app on the 6 GB emulator by `scripts/eval_phone.py`. Every answer was checked against the demo data. The two
students are Asha, in Intermediate, and Kavita, in Pain management. There were six runs, and after each one, reading
the answers found what the automatic checks had not. The fixes went into code wherever they could:

- **Gemma read averages against the scale's numbers** ("from 1 not yet to 3 steady" for 2.0 and 3.7). Every mood and
  average reaction now reaches it in words.
- **It took a count for a rating** ("you rated it a 7"), and practice in class for practice alone. The summary names
  counts as counts, and keeps the items practised in class and alone apart.
- **It wrote English into Marathi answers:** the tool's English asana names, and glosses of its own ("नितंब (hips)").
  Hindi and Marathi now get Devanagari names, and English in brackets is removed from their replies in code.
- **It gave Kavita's next class as Monday** when it was the next day, Friday. The schedule now says which class is
  next, and the check needs the class's weekday and time.
- **It offered to "check with" the teacher.** A rule now says it cannot reach anyone.

The last run passed 21 of 21 automatic checks, and 20 of 21 by reading. The one failure is in Marathi: the facts are
right, but the answer calls Setu Bandhasana a pranayama. Five held-out questions, written after the last change and
asked once, were all answered correctly. A reply takes about 16 s on the emulator's CPU.

## The release

- **The APK.** `scripts/build_app.sh --release` builds `satt-chitt-anand-1.0-arm64-v8a.apk`:
  - versionName 1.0;
  - the label "Satt Chitt Anand", सत्·चित्·आनन्द in Hindi and Marathi;
  - arm64-v8a only, no plain HTTP, and no server-address field;
  - the live web app's address;
  - signed with the studio's key, made by this first release build and kept out of git with that address.
- **R8 and LiteRT-LM.** The shrunk release was also built for the emulator, against the dev backend and signed with
  the debug key, so that it installed over the debug app and kept the downloaded model. On it:
  - Gemma started, and its tools, which LiteRT-LM finds by reflection, answered;
  - the asana, schedule and practice answers were word for word the debug build's;
  - Today and Practice read the server's JSON.

  R8 warns that it cannot read this newer Kotlin's metadata. The shrunk app above ran all the same.
- **The live web app, read only.** The same release built for the emulator, with the live address and the studio's key,
  opened on a 2 GB emulator. Its first screen read the studio from the live web app in 5 s, and in Hindi in 2 s, with
  no server field. Nothing was signed up or logged in, so nothing was written to the studio's Sheet. The live web app
  still runs the v0.7 bundle (its Hindi tagline still reads "आनंद") until the owner pastes v1.0's
  ([steps](../../appsscript/README.md#update-later)).
